# PEN-Plus NCD Monitoring App — Video Script

**Target runtime:** about 9 minutes 
**Format:** tablet screen recording in landscape, with voice-over or presenter narration

## 0:00– 0:14 — Welcome and Selecting your identity

**On screen:** Tap the initials in the top-right corner to open the setup screen. Select the demo user.

**Narration:**
"Welcome. In this video, I'll walk through the PEN-Plus NCD monitoring app and show how a mentor uses it to record and follow up on a mentee's clinical competencies.

We'll go from the home screen to selecting a mentee, planning and scoring a session, reviewing it before saving, and following progress over time.

The app is built for field use on a tablet, including when there's no connection (but any device can be used including laptops, computers, cellphones etc where necessary). It organizes assessments into journeys. A journey is one mentee's progress on one clinical tool over time. Each mentorship visit is recorded as a session in that journey."

"On first use, the mentor selects their name from the clinician list, so the sessions they record are attributed to them. This is a name selection, not a full login. You can change it any time by tapping your initials."

## 0:14–0:44 — Home screen and navigation

**On screen:** The **New Session** and **My Mentees** buttons, the recent sessions list, and the sync status line. Then point to the header.

**Narration:**

"This is the home screen. Both main buttons open the mentee list, because every session starts with choosing a mentee. Recent sessions are listed below, and the status line shows whether the device is syncing.

The header is the same on every screen. **Back** on the left always takes you to the previous screen."

## 0:44–1:16 — Finding and selecting a mentee

**On screen:** Tap **My Mentees**. Search for Mentee A and open their profile.

**Narration:**

"This is the mentee list. You can search by name or facility, and add new mentees here. The mentee card shows summary details e.g Name, profession, district, facility and how many sessions they have done so far including gaps logged if any.


## 1:16–2:00 — Finding and selecting a mentee

**On screen:** go to **Mentees Search bar**. Search for Mentee Demo Mentee 051 and open their profile.

I'll open this mentee's profile. It lists their evaluation journeys, one per tool. Each shows the number of sessions, the last visit, the phase or competency status, and any open gaps.

Journeys are kept separate by tool. A mentee can work on diabetes and respiratory competencies at the same time, and each has its own history."

## 2:00–2:05 — Choosing a clinical tool

**On screen:** Tap **Start New Session** to open the tool picker. Select **Echocardiogram** since it  has the least items.

**Narration:**

"To start an assessment, tap **Start New Session** and choose the tool.

If a journey already exists for that tool, this visit continues it. Once a mentee reaches competency, the journey closes. Reopening it needs a short reason, so the record shows why the mentee was assessed again.

I'll continue the Echocardiogram."

## 2:00–2:42 — Planning the assessment

**On screen:** The planning step. Point out the date field, the item sections, **Select all** and **Clear**, the counselling section, and the grey advanced item. Select all a few items. Tap **Confirm plan**.

**Narration:**

"Before scoring, the mentor plans what will be observed today. Items are grouped into the same sections as the paper tool.

You don't have to observe every item at every visit. Select what's relevant today. The general counselling competencies are included in every session, whatever the tool.

Items shaded grey are advanced competencies. They aren't required for a mentee to be considered competent, but they can still be assessed.

I'll confirm the plan and move on to observation."

## 2:42–4:14 — Recording observations and scores

**On screen:** The observation list. Score all planned items at random scores. Show the meaning chip under an item, and the Improved indicator. Mark one **counselling** item **N/A**.

**Narration:**

"Here is the observation list for today's plan. Each item has six buttons: scores 1 to 5, and N/A.

When I tap a score, its meaning appears underneath, in the same colour as the legend. Here, 4 means the mentee demonstrates advanced competency . N/A means the item couldn't be evaluated during this visit.

The app records the mentee's score only. There's no separate mentor or autonomy score.

Because this mentee has been assessed before, each item shows the previous score and its date. After I score, the app shows whether the mentee has improved, regressed, or stayed the same.

Each item also has an optional note, for what you observed or why an item couldn't be evaluated."


**Narration:**

"A visit can include more than one observation round, for example the ward in the morning and the outpatient clinic in the afternoon. Rounds belong to the same visit, and I can switch between them at any time."

**On screen:** Scroll to the action bar. **Review session** is disabled, and the bar says "Echocardiogram counts" showing it has to be filled in.


## 4:14–4:39 — Recording Echocardiogram activity
**On screen:** Echocardiogram activity card with no values recorded. Mark **Echocardiogram performed under supervision** with a 2 item and **Echocardiogram performed under supervision** with a 1. Then fill in session notes. and then click **Review session**.


"Notice that **Review session** is disabled, and the bar tells me why: I haven't selected a mentorship phase. Tapping the message takes me straight to it. I'll choose **Ongoing**, and Review is ready."

## 4:39–5:21 — Reviewing and saving the session

**On screen:** Tap **Review session**. Show the outcome card at the top first. Then show the summary row, an average tile, the item chips, the dashed **Not scored** item, and a change marker. Tap **Save Session** in the bottom bar.

**Narration:**

"Before saving, the mentor reviews the session. The first thing the review shows is the outcome. Here it says **Not yet Competent**, because some items have scores below 4 . If any were all 4's and 5's, the review would be showing **Basic Competency**.

.Here, the tool average falls in the Basic band. Every item shows its score and what it means, plus whether it changed since the last session.

Sometimes an item is marked **Not Scored**  I planned it but didn't score it, and the review makes that easy to spot. If it's an advanced item, it doesn't stop the mentee reaching Basic Competent. If something needs fixing, **Back to edit** returns me to the form with everything still in place.

When it all looks right, I tap **Save Session**. It's saved on the tablet straight away, and syncs to the server when the device is online."

## 5:21–5:54 — Journey history and competency status

**On screen:** After saving, the app returns to the mentee profile, where the journey now is now on top shows **Ongoing**. Open the journey. Explain a bit on how the summary card shows how many sessions the journey has, and when it was done also showing the latest round of that day/session.

**Narration:**

"After saving, the app returns to the mentee's profile, and the journey now shows Ongoing.

The session list shows every visit in date order. I can open any session to see its full scores. If a mistake was saved, **Edit** reopens it with the original entries.". ## 5:54–6:50 — Grouped Competency items

**On screen:** Click on  **Start New Session** and choose Diabetes Mellitus tool. In the plan mode Scroll down to show how the competency items are grouped exactly as in the tool document. Show that one can select all items within a group, clear them aor select a few items they want to deal it on that session.
**Narration:**

"The software aligns well with the paper based monitoring tools. Lets go to Diabetes Mellitus tool - there are about 37 competency items but they are all grouped into their respective categories. One can select all items within a category or a few depending on how they are conducting their monitoring.
Since we have already done the scoring, we are not going to repeat the process, so lets just click the back button to end the planning
"

## 6:50–7:31 — Sync and wrap-up

**On screen:** Tap the sync button in the header to open **Sync Data**. Point out **Sync All**. Don't run **Storage Cleanup**. Return home.

**Narration:**

"The app syncs automatically whenever the tablet is online. Work is saved on the tablet first, so nothing is lost while offline. This sync page is a fallback. It shows when data last synced, and **Sync All** pulls the latest users, sessions and districts from the server at once. Before leaving a facility, it's worth checking here that your sessions have synced.

That's the core workflow: choose a mentee and a tool, plan, observe, review, save, and follow progress over time. Thank you for watching. We'd welcome your feedback on anything that should be clearer or easier to use."
