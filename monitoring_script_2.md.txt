# PEN-Plus NCD Monitoring App — Video Script

**Target runtime:** about 17 minutes including the optional Echocardiogram segment and the gaps bonus, recorded as two parts of under 10 minutes
**Format:** tablet screen recording in landscape, with voice-over or presenter narration
**Bonus:** a standalone gaps segment of under 1 minute at the end. It can stay in the main video or be published as its own short clip.

## Before recording

Use a demo account and fictional data only. Don't show real patient or clinician information.

Prepare this demo data in advance:

- **Mentee A is Demo Mentee 051**, with an in-progress **Chronic Liver Disease** journey. It has three earlier sessions with all 15 items planned. Only L1, L2, L5 and L6 are below 4, all at 3.
  - The app requires **every planned item to score 4 or 5 in the latest session**. So in the demo session, plan all items and score all 14 required items at 4 or 5. Scoring L1, L2, L5 and L6 at 4 shows "Improved", and the review page says "Saving will mark this journey Basic Competent".
  - Leave only **L10**, the advanced item, unscored. Mark N/A only on a **counselling** item. Either of these on a required item would block competency.
  - The liver journey has no open gap. Log one before recording if you want to show **Resolve** in the gaps bonus.
  - Backup: Demo Mentee 050's liver journey is also four items away, but its scores sit at 1 to 3.
- **Mentee B** has no sessions yet.
- **One closed journey** for the reopen step. Demo Mentee 051 already has two: Chronic Kidney Disease and Sickle Cell Disease.
- **Optional:** an **Echocardiogram** journey with two or three earlier visits that have counts recorded. No session has counts yet, so record a few first. Demo Mentee 050's Echocardiogram journey, with four sessions and an open gap, is a good base.

Chronic Liver Disease suits the demo. It has 15 items, which is manageable on screen, and L10 is an advanced item.

Also check that:

- the tablet is online, so the sync indicator shows as active,
- the app is in light mode,
- notifications are off on the tablet.

## 0:00–1:00 — Welcome and purpose

**On screen:** The home screen.

**Narration:**

"Welcome. In this video, I'll walk through the PEN-Plus NCD monitoring app and show how a mentor uses it to record and follow up on a mentee's clinical competencies.

We'll go from the home screen to selecting a mentee, planning and scoring a session, reviewing it before saving, and following progress over time.

The app is built for field use on a tablet, including when there's no connection. It organizes assessments into journeys. A journey is one mentee's progress on one clinical tool over time. Each mentorship visit is recorded as a session in that journey."

## 1:00–1:45 — Selecting your identity

**On screen:** Tap the initials in the top-right corner to open the setup screen. Select the demo user.

**Narration:**

"On first use, the mentor selects their name from the clinician list, so the sessions they record are attributed to them. This is a name selection, not a full login. You can change it any time by tapping your initials."

## 1:45–2:30 — Home screen and navigation

**On screen:** The **New Session** and **My Mentees** buttons, the recent sessions list, and the sync status line. Then point to the header.

**Narration:**

"This is the home screen. Both main buttons open the mentee list, because every session starts with choosing a mentee. Recent sessions are listed below, and the status line shows whether the device is syncing.

The header is the same on every screen. **Back** on the left always takes you to the previous screen."

## 2:30–4:00 — Finding and selecting a mentee

**On screen:** Tap **My Mentees**. Search for Mentee A and open their profile.

**Narration:**

"This is the mentee list. You can search by name or facility, and add new mentees here.

I'll open this mentee's profile. It lists their evaluation journeys, one per tool. Each shows the number of sessions, the last visit, the phase or competency status, and any open gaps.

Journeys are kept separate by tool. A mentee can work on diabetes and respiratory competencies at the same time, and each has its own history."

## 4:00–5:00 — Choosing a clinical tool

**On screen:** Tap **Start New Session** to open the tool picker. Point out the closed journey and its "tap to reopen" note, but don't reopen it. Select **Chronic Liver Disease**.

**Narration:**

"To start an assessment, tap **Start New Session** and choose the tool.

If a journey already exists for that tool, this visit continues it. Once a mentee reaches competency, the journey closes. Reopening it needs a short reason, so the record shows why the mentee was assessed again.

I'll continue the Chronic Liver Disease journey."

## 5:00–7:00 — Planning the assessment

**On screen:** The planning step. Point out the date field, the item sections, **Select all** and **Clear**, the counselling section, and the grey advanced item. Select all 15 items. **Don't choose a phase yet.** Tap **Confirm plan**.

**Narration:**

"Before scoring, the mentor plans what will be observed today. Items are grouped into the same sections as the paper tool.

You don't have to observe every item at every visit. Select what's relevant today. The general counselling competencies are included in every session, whatever the tool.

Items shaded grey are advanced competencies. They aren't required for a mentee to be considered competent, but they can still be assessed.

I'll confirm the plan and move on to observation."

## 7:00–10:30 — Recording observations and scores

**On screen:** The observation list. Score all 14 required items at 4 or 5, including L1, L2, L5 and L6 at 4. Show the meaning chip under an item, and the Improved indicator. Mark one **counselling** item **N/A** and add an item note. Leave **L10** unscored.

**Narration:**

"Here is the observation list for today's plan. Each item has six buttons: scores 1 to 5, and N/A.

When I tap a score, its meaning appears underneath, in the same colour as the legend. Here, 4 means the mentee demonstrates advanced competency. N/A means the item couldn't be evaluated during this visit.

The app records the mentee's score only. There's no separate mentor or autonomy score.

Because this mentee has been assessed before, each item shows the previous score and its date. After I score, the app shows whether the mentee has improved, regressed, or stayed the same.

Each item also has an optional note, for what you observed or why an item couldn't be evaluated."

**On screen:** Tap **Add round**, rename it, score a couple of items, then switch back to the first round.

**Narration:**

"A visit can include more than one observation round, for example the ward in the morning and the outpatient clinic in the afternoon. I'll add a second round and score a couple of items. Rounds belong to the same visit, and I can switch between them at any time."

**On screen:** Scroll to the action bar. **Review session** is disabled, and the bar says "Select a mentorship phase". Tap the message. The page scrolls to the phase buttons. Select **Ongoing**.

**Narration:**

"Notice that **Review session** is disabled, and the bar tells me why: I haven't selected a mentorship phase. Tapping the message takes me straight to it. I'll choose **Ongoing**, and Review is ready."

## 10:30–12:30 — Reviewing and saving the session

**On screen:** Tap **Review session**. Show the outcome card at the top first. Then show the summary row, an average tile, the item chips, the dashed **Not scored** item, and a change marker. Tap **Save Session** in the bottom bar.

**Narration:**

"Before saving, the mentor reviews the session. The first thing the review shows is the outcome. Here it says **Saving will mark this journey Basic Competent**, because every required item now scores 4 or 5. If any were still below 4, they'd be listed here instead.

Below that are the averages, shown with their meaning. Here, the tool average falls in the Advanced band. Every item shows its score and what it means, plus whether it changed since the last session.

This item, L10, is marked **Not scored**. I planned it but didn't score it, and the review makes that easy to spot. Because it's an advanced item, it doesn't stop the mentee reaching Basic Competent. If something needs fixing, **Back to edit** returns me to the form with everything still in place.

When it all looks right, I tap **Save Session**. It's saved on the tablet straight away, and syncs to the server when the device is online."

## 12:30–14:00 — Journey history and competency status

**On screen:** After saving, the app returns to the mentee profile, where the journey now shows **Basic Competent**. Open the journey. Show the competency banner, the summary card, and the session list. Open a session to show its details and the **Edit** button.

**Narration:**

"After saving, the app returns to the mentee's profile, and the journey now shows Basic Competent. Inside the journey, the banner confirms what the review predicted: **Basic Competency Reached**.

A journey reaches Basic Competent when every required item planned in the journey scores 4 or 5 in the most recent session. It becomes **Fully Competent** when the advanced items reach 4 or 5 as well. Once competent, the journey closes.

The session list shows every visit in date order. I can open any session to see its full scores. If a mistake was saved, **Edit** reopens it with the original entries."

## Optional, 14:00–15:00 — Echocardiogram activity

Include this segment if the audience includes cardiac mentors. It needs the Echocardiogram journey prepared under "Before recording".

**On screen:** Go back to the mentee list and open **Demo Mentee 050**. Start a new **Echocardiogram** session. In the plan, keep only E8 and the counselling items, and choose a phase. Score E8. Scroll to **Echocardiogram activity · whole visit** and use the plus buttons to enter 6 performed and 5 correct. Show the accuracy line, then scroll up to E8 and show its accuracy note. Review and save, then open the Echocardiogram journey and show the **Supervised echocardiograms** card.

**Narration:**

"One tool has an extra step. For Echocardiogram, the mentor also records two counts from the paper tool: how many echocardiograms the mentee performed under supervision, and how many reached a correct final diagnosis. It's one total for the whole visit, entered with these plus and minus buttons.

The app works out the diagnostic accuracy, here 5 of 6, or 83 percent. The same figure appears under item E8, correctly identifying the diagnosis, as supporting information while scoring. It doesn't set the score.

On the journey page, the counts add up across visits, so you can see total supervised scans and how accuracy is trending."

## 15:00–16:00 — Sync and wrap-up

**On screen:** Tap the sync button in the header to open **Sync Data**. Point out **Sync All**. Don't run **Storage Cleanup**. Return home.

**Narration:**

"The app syncs automatically whenever the tablet is online. Work is saved on the tablet first, so nothing is lost while offline. This sync page is a fallback. It shows when data last synced, and **Sync All** pulls the latest users, sessions and districts from the server at once. Before leaving a facility, it's worth checking here that your sessions have synced.

That's the core workflow: choose a mentee and a tool, plan, observe, review, save, and follow progress over time. Thank you for watching. We'd welcome your feedback on anything that should be clearer or easier to use."

## Bonus, 16:00–17:00 — Mentorship gaps

Under one minute. It can stay at the end of the main video, or be published as its own short clip.

**On screen:** Open Mentee A's Chronic Liver Disease journey and scroll to **Gaps**. Tap **Log Gap**, fill in a fictional example, and tap **Log Gap** to save. Then tap **Resolve** on the existing open gap, add a short note, and tap **Mark Resolved**.

**Narration:**

"One more feature: mentorship gaps. A gap is something the mentee needs to work on. It belongs to the journey, not a single visit, so it can be followed up over time.

To log one, I describe what I saw and choose the domains, such as knowledge or clinical skills. I note whether it was addressed today, when it will be covered, and the supervision level recommended.

Once it's been addressed, I tap **Resolve**, add a short note on how, and mark it resolved. Open gaps also show on the mentee's profile, so nothing gets forgotten between visits."

## Recording plan for Clueso

The Clueso free tier allows 10 minutes per video, so record the demo as **two parts** and merge them afterwards. Each part is planned for about 8 to 9 minutes of narration, which leaves room for pauses.

| Part | Script sections | Narration length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part 1 | Welcome, up to the end of the first scoring block | about 9 minutes |
| Part 2 | Adding a round, up to the end of the gaps bonus | about 8 minutes |

### How to record

- **Record without speaking.** Paste the narration into Clueso afterwards and let it generate the voiceover, so your clicks don't need to match your speaking pace.
- **Move slowly.** Pause for about two seconds on each new screen and after each tap. Clueso can trim pauses, but it can't add time you didn't record.
- **On the tablet**, use Android's built-in screen recorder from Quick Settings. Turn on **Show taps** in Developer options so viewers can see where you tap. Turn on Do Not Disturb.
- **Keep the app open between parts.** Stop the Part 1 recording without leaving the session form, keep the screen awake, then start Part 2 on the same screen. The scored session is not saved yet, so leaving the form or reloading would lose it.

### Part 1 shot list, about 9 minutes

Start on the home screen, signed in as someone other than the demo user.

1. **Welcome.** Stay on the home screen for about 20 seconds while the intro plays.
2. **Identity.** Tap your initials, search for the demo user, and select them. You land back on home.
3. **Home and navigation.** Point out New Session, My Mentees, recent sessions and the sync line. Then hold on the header, showing the Back button area.
4. **Mentee.** Tap **My Mentees**, search "051", and open **Demo Mentee 051**. Hold on the journey list, showing the badges.
5. **Tool picker.** Tap **Start New Session**. Hold on the closed Chronic Kidney Disease or Sickle Cell row with "tap to reopen", but don't tap it. Select **Chronic Liver Disease**.
6. **Planning.** Scroll through the sections. Show **Select all** and **Clear**, the counselling section, and the grey L10 row. Make sure all 15 items are selected. **Don't choose a phase.** Tap **Confirm plan**.
7. **Scoring.** Score the 14 required items at 4 or 5. Score L1, L2, L5 and L6 at 4, so "Improved" appears. Pause on one item to show the meaning chip and the previous score. Mark one **counselling** item N/A and type a short item note. Leave **L10** unscored.
8. **Stop recording** here, still on the observation screen.

### Part 2 shot list, about 8 minutes

Start on the same observation screen, right where Part 1 ended.

1. **Rounds.** Tap **Add round**, rename it "Outpatient clinic", score two items, then switch back to Round 1.
2. **Disabled Review.** Scroll down to the action bar. Hold on "Select a mentorship phase". Tap it, let the page scroll, and select **Ongoing**.
3. **Review.** Tap **Review session**. Hold on the outcome card, "Saving will mark this journey Basic Competent". Scroll slowly past the averages, the item chips, an "Improved" marker and the dashed **Not scored** L10. Tap **Save Session**.
4. **Journey.** On the mentee profile, show the liver journey now marked Basic Competent. Open it, hold on the banner, and scroll the session list. Open one session and show the **Edit** button, then go Back.
5. **Echocardiogram, optional.** Follow the Echocardiogram segment's on-screen notes with Demo Mentee 050.
6. **Sync and wrap-up.** Tap the sync button in the header. Hold on **Sync All**, but don't tap **Storage Cleanup**. Go back home.
7. **Gaps bonus.** Open Demo Mentee 051's liver journey and scroll to **Gaps**. Tap **Log Gap**, fill in a fictional example and save it. Then tap **Resolve** on a gap, add a note, and tap **Mark Resolved**. If there was no open gap beforehand, resolve the one you just logged.
8. **Stop recording.**

### After recording

- In Clueso, paste each part's narration from the sections above into its own project and generate the voiceover.
- If a part runs over 10 minutes, shorten pauses first, then trim the home screen or sync narration.
- Merge Part 1 and Part 2 into the final video.

## Recording tips

- Record on the tablet itself in landscape. That is where the layout has been tuned.
- Rehearse the planning and scoring once with the demo data, so the review page shows "Saving will mark this journey Basic Competent". Reset the data before the real take.
- Stay with Mentee A and the Chronic Liver Disease tool for most of the video. Switching often is harder to follow.
- Pause for a second after each tap so viewers can read the screen.
- Don't show real names, patient details, credentials, or server addresses.
- Don't run **Storage Cleanup** on camera. It permanently removes old synced sessions from the tablet.
