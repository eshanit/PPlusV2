# PenPlus NCD: Monitoring App Presentation Script

**Length:** about 18 minutes, then questions
**Signed in as:** Evaluator User
**Format:** **Show** is what to do on screen. **Say** is roughly what to say. Keep it conversational, not word for word.

---

## Before you start (5 minutes before)

- [ ] Clear the browser's site data for the monitoring app, reload, and pick **Evaluator User** as the identity.
- [ ] Check that the home screen sync line says **Up to date**.
- [ ] Open the reporting dashboard in a second tab, ready for the next part.
- [ ] Use the tablet view or a narrow window. The app is designed for a tablet.
- [ ] Part 3 completes Tendai's journey live. To run the demo again afterwards, clear and re-seed the data. Don't rehearse Part 3 on the data you'll present with.

---

## Part 1: Opening (1 minute)

**Say:**
> "Thank you for the feedback on the first version. Today I'll show the field app as a mentor would use it on a tablet during a visit, and I'll point out each change you asked for as we reach it.
>
> I've loaded ten example mentees. Each one shows a different situation you'll meet in the field: someone close to competency, someone struggling, a mentee handed over from another mentor, and so on. The names are made up."

---

## Part 2: The home screen (2 minutes)

**Show:** the home screen. Scroll slowly from top to bottom.

**Say:**
> "This is the first thing a mentor sees. There are two main actions: start a **New Session**, or browse **My Mentees**.
>
> Under that, **Continue where you left off** lists journeys the mentor has visited that aren't finished yet, so the next visit is one tap away."

**Show:** the **Competencies not yet evaluated** card.

**Say:**
> "This card is new. It answers a question from your feedback about seeing all the competencies across a journey. For each mentee, it counts the **required competencies that have never been scored**. Until those are scored, the mentee can't be declared competent, so they're easy to miss without a reminder."

**Show:** switch from **Mine** to **All evaluators**.

**Say:**
> "Nurses and doctors move between facilities a lot. With **All evaluators**, a mentor can see journeys other mentors started and continue where they left off. We'll do exactly that next."

**Show:** point to **Open gaps to follow up**, then to the sync line at the bottom.

**Say:**
> "Here are the gaps this mentor logged that are still open. At the bottom is the sync status. The app works fully offline and sends everything once a connection is back."

---

## Part 3: Picking up a colleague's mentee (2 minutes)

**Show:** with **All evaluators** selected, tap **Nyasha Dube · Sickle Cell Disease**.

**Say:**
> "Nyasha was being mentored by Blessing Nyathi, who has since moved. I've never seen Nyasha, but I can see her whole history."

**Show:** the **Competency coverage** card on the journey page.

**Say:**
> "This card tells me straight away: last evaluated in August by Blessing. Twelve required competencies have never been evaluated, so I'll plan the next visit around those.
>
> It also flags **S5**. It was scored 2 at the first visit and hasn't been looked at since. A low score that nobody follows up is easy to lose track of, so it's listed under **Not evaluated recently**.
>
> Competencies already at 4 or 5 are never flagged. Once a mentee has achieved something, nobody needs to re-rate it."

---

## Part 4: A live session that reaches competency (6 minutes)

This is the centrepiece. Tendai is one competency away from being competent in Diabetes.

**Show:** tap **My Mentees**, search **Tendai**, open **Tendai Moyo**.

**Say:**
> "Each mentee has a journey per tool. Tendai's Diabetes journey has four sessions. The card shows who did the last visit and how far she has to go."

**Show:** open the Diabetes journey, then start a new session.

### 4a. Date, phase and planning

**Show:** the session header, the evaluation date and the phase selector.

**Say:**
> "The session opens with the tool, the mentee and her facility. I set the date and the mentorship phase. Any open gaps from earlier visits are listed here, so I'm reminded what we said we'd work on."

**Show:** the **Choose the competencies evaluated** screen. Scroll through the sections.

**Say:**
> "As on paper, the competencies are grouped into sections. I tick the ones I actually observed during the visit, based on the patients I saw with Tendai. I don't have to score everything at every visit.
>
> The green **Achieved** labels mark competencies she has already scored 4 or 5 on, so I can skip them. Only **D23** is left."

> **Tip:** if you have time, also open Tatenda Sibanda's Respiratory journey. On this screen it shows amber labels: **Not yet evaluated** and **Not rated for 3 sessions**.

**Show:** select **D23**, then tap **Continue to scoring**.

### 4b. Scoring

**Show:** open the score key, the legend table.

**Say:**
> "Here is the scoring key from the paper tool. You asked for the **Mentor** row. Each score now shows what it means for the mentee and for the mentor. For example, a 4 means the mentee 'demonstrates advanced competency' and, for the mentor, 'I needed to be there just in case'. We still record a single score; the mentor wording just explains what it means."

**Show:** tap a score on D23, say 4. Point to the coloured key that appears with both meanings.

**Say:**
> "When I tap a score, the meaning appears right under the competency, for both the mentee and the mentor, so there's no guessing. For scores 1 to 5 I can add a note.
>
> If I choose **N/A**, there's no note box, as you suggested. There's nothing to explain."

**Show:** scroll to the General Counselling competencies, DC1 to DC9. Score a few.

**Say:**
> "The general counselling competencies come with every disease tool, except the Echocardiogram, where you asked for them to be removed."

### 4c. Rounds (mention briefly)

**Show:** point to **Add round**. You don't have to add one.

**Say:**
> "If I observe Tendai again later the same day, on a ward round and then in clinic, for example, I can add a round. Each round can cover different competencies from the same tool."

### 4d. Gaps instead of notes

**Show:** scroll to **Gaps identified in Round 1**, tap **Log a gap**, fill in a short gap, and save it.

**Say:**
> "Session notes are replaced by gaps, as you asked. A gap is logged for the round where it was seen. It records the area (knowledge, clinical skills, communication and so on), whether it will be covered during this mentorship, and the recommended level of supervision. Gaps stay with the journey until someone resolves them, even if a different mentor takes over."

### 4e. Review and save

**Show:** tap **Review session**. Scroll through the review page.

**Say:**
> "Before saving, I can review everything: each score with its meaning, the counselling scores and the gaps. If something is wrong, I go back and change it."

**Show:** save. Return to the journey and point to the competency banner.

**Say:**
> "Tendai is now **Basic Competent** in Diabetes. Every required competency in the tool has reached 4 or 5. The rule is the one we agreed: the latest score on each competency counts, wherever in the journey it was given. The journey is now closed, so nobody can add a session to it by mistake."

## Part 5: Seeing the whole journey (3 minutes)

**Show:** open **Chipo Mutasa · Chronic Liver Disease**.

**Say:**
> "Chipo's journey shows how field work really goes. Each visit covered different competencies, and some visits had two rounds."

**Show:** switch from **Sessions** to **Competency grid**.

**Say:**
> "This is the paper tool's layout. Competencies run down the side, sessions run across the top, and each cell holds the score from that visit. The last columns show her latest score and whether each competency is achieved. Tap any score to see what it means."

**Show:** back on **Sessions**, scroll to the gaps. Resolve one open gap and add a short resolution note.

**Say:**
> "Gaps are followed up here. When one has been addressed, the mentor resolves it with a note, and the date is recorded for reporting."

---

## Part 6: Echocardiogram (1.5 minutes)

**Show:** open **Kudzai Banda · Echocardiogram**.

**Say:**
> "The Echocardiogram tool has two counts on paper: echos performed under supervision and echos with a correct final diagnosis. The app collects them with simple plus and minus buttons, and checks that correct diagnoses can't be more than echos performed."

**Show:** the echo activity card on the journey page.

**Say:**
> "Across Kudzai's visits, diagnostic accuracy rose from one in three to seven in eight. There are no counselling competencies on this tool, as requested."

---

## Part 7: Competent journeys and the advanced items (1.5 minutes)

**Show:** open **Rudo Chirwa**.

**Say:**
> "Rudo has two finished journeys. Hypertension is **Fully Competent**. CKD is **Basic Competent**: every required competency is achieved, but the advanced one, H15, the grey item on paper, isn't yet. As the paper tool says, advanced items aren't needed to be considered competent, but we still track them and report both levels."

**Show:** try to start a new session on a closed journey and point to the message that blocks it.

---

## Part 8: Offline and sync (1 minute)

**Show:** open the **Sync** page.

**Say:**
> "Everything is saved on the tablet first, so poor connectivity never stops a session. When there's a connection, data syncs automatically, and this page shows what has been sent and what is waiting. The same data feeds the reporting dashboard, which I'll show next."

---

## Close (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "To recap the changes you asked for: the Mentor row in the scoring key, no notes for N/A, gaps logged per round instead of notes, counselling removed from the Echocardiogram, and competency reached once every required item is at 4 or 5. On top of that, you can now see every competency across a journey, including what has been missed, and pick up a colleague's mentee.
>
> Now let's look at what this data shows on the reporting side."

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A mentee is missing | Open **Sync** and wait for it to finish, or reload the page. |
| Tendai is already competent | Part 4 was run earlier. Show Tatenda's plan screen instead, and use Rudo for the competency banner. |
| The **All evaluators** list is empty | You are still on **Mine**. Switch the toggle. |
| A question about the rules | Achieved means the latest score is 4 or 5. Basic Competent means all required items are achieved. Fully Competent means advanced items are achieved too. N/A never replaces a score. |
